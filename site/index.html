What you can do

Endless worlds

Every world is generated from a seed: plains, forests, deserts, snowy peaks, oceans and winding caves full of ore.

Build anything

26 block types: wood, bricks, glass, colored cloth, gold and more. Break and place blocks instantly in creative mode.

Day & night

Watch the sun set over the ocean and the stars come out. Clouds drift by, and you can swim and fly.

Made for phones

A joystick, touch-to-look, and tap or hold to build and break. Plays in full-screen landscape mode.

Auto-save

Your world is saved on your device every 20 seconds and whenever you pause, so you can pick up where you left off.

Plays offline

The Android app needs no internet connection. The whole game is small, and every texture is drawn in code.

Install on Android

  1. 1

    Download

    Tap Download APK on your phone. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Open the file

    Open BlocksCraft.apk from your notifications or the Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Allow the install

    If Android asks, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

  4. 4

    Play!

    Tap Install, then open BlocksCraft from your home screen.

Requires Android 6.0 or newer. New versions install over the old one, and your world is kept. On iPhone or a computer? Play in your browser instead.
